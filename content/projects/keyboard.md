This started over a year ago as a 2-key keyboard — a copy-paste gadget. I
designed the PCB myself in JLCPCB, soldered it together, and made the housing
in Onshape, 3D printed. Small, but the full loop: board design, assembly,
enclosure.

Recently I expanded the idea into a full keyboard: a custom PCB with
dimensions fit to my own requirements. The switches are soldered, the
firmware is written with ZMK to match how I actually work (config repo
linked above), keycaps are being designed through YUZUKeycaps, and a
CAD housing case is next.
